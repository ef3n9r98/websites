Unofficial reproductions

The decisions in CaseLawAtlas are unofficial reproductions of decisions of the Supreme Court of Canada, the Federal Court of Appeal, the Federal Court, the Tax Court of Canada and the Court Martial Appeal Court of Canada. They are not official versions. CaseLawAtlas is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these courts or by the Government of Canada. The text may contain errors and may not reflect later corrections, anonymizations or removals. Before relying on a decision, check the official version on the court's website; every decision in the app links to it.

Not legal advice

CaseLawAtlas is a research and reading tool. Nothing in it is legal advice, and using it doesn't create a lawyer–client relationship. The sections shown below a decision's full text (such as the outcome, case in brief, held and the bench) are extracted automatically and may be incomplete or wrong. For advice about your situation, talk to a lawyer.

Data source

Sean Rehaag, Simon Wallace & Clifford McCarten, “A2AJ Canadian Case Law”, Access to Algorithmic Justice (A2AJ).

All decision text and case details in CaseLawAtlas come from this dataset. A2AJ's own work on it is under the MIT licence, and A2AJ makes no warranty that it is complete or accurate. Each decision is also subject to its court's terms, below.
