Support

CaseLawAtlas lets you find and read about 62,000 decisions of the Supreme Court of Canada, the Federal Court of Appeal, the Federal Court, the Tax Court of Canada and the Court Martial Appeal Court of Canada, from 1877 to today. New decisions are added as the courts publish them.

Reporting a problem with a decision

If a decision should be anonymized, is under a publication ban, has been withdrawn, or shows the wrong text, open it in the app and tap Report a problem with this decision at the end of the case. That sends an email that identifies the case exactly. You can also email the address above with the citation, for example R. v. Jordan, 2016 SCC 27.

Common questions

A case won't open. Decisions are downloaded the first time you open them, so you need an internet connection. Cases you've opened before work offline.

A recent decision is missing. The app checks for new decisions each time it opens. A decision can take a few days to appear after the court publishes it.

Is this legal advice? No. CaseLawAtlas is an unofficial reproduction for research and reading. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the courts or the Government of Canada. For the official version, use the Open Official Version link on each case.
