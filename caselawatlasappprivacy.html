Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

CaseLawAtlas is an iPhone app for reading and searching decisions of five Canadian federal courts. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • the cases you save and the cases you've recently viewed;
  • your list settings, such as sort order;
  • that you've accepted the first-launch disclaimer;
  • a copy of the case list and of each decision you've opened or saved, so they load quickly and work offline.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Information sent over the network

To show the list of cases and the decisions you open, the app downloads them from our server, which is hosted by Supabase. These requests contain no account, name, email address, advertising identifier or other personal information. Like most web services, the server keeps standard technical logs of requests, which include your IP address, the time and the file requested. We use these logs to run and secure the service, and to count how many times each decision is downloaded in total. Those totals are all we keep from the logs: they contain no IP address or anything else that identifies you or your device. We don't combine the logs with other information. Supabase processes them on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

When you tap a link to an official court website, or to the A2AJ website, that site's own privacy policy applies.

If you email us, for example with "Report a problem with this decision", we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to respond and to act on your report.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, contacts, location or any other personal information.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share data.
  • The app isn't directed at children.

Decisions that mention you

Decisions are reproduced from the courts' public websites through the A2AJ Canadian Case Law dataset. If a decision in the app should be anonymized, is under a publication ban or has been withdrawn, use "Report a problem with this decision" in the app or email us at eugenefds3@gmail.com, and we'll remove or correct it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
